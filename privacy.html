Privacy Policy

Adam's Assistant, operated by Adam Zeringue · Effective October 20, 2026

This policy covers text messages sent by Adam's Assistant, a personal automated scheduling assistant operated by Adam Zeringue, an individual.

Information collected

  • Your mobile phone number and the name Adam knows you by.
  • The text messages the assistant sends you and the replies you send back.
  • Whether you have agreed to receive texts, or opted out.

How it is used

This information is used only to confirm or arrange get-togethers with Adam, such as agreeing on a date, time, and place, and to honor opt-out requests. It is not used for marketing or advertising.

Service providers

To deliver texts and read replies, the assistant relies on service providers that process messages on Adam's behalf: a text messaging provider (Twilio) that delivers messages, and an AI service that reads replies and drafts responses. These providers handle messages only to run the assistant.

No sharing for marketing

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Storage and deletion

Information is stored on Adam's personal computer. You can ask to have your information deleted at any time by emailing the address below.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help.
