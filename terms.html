Adam's Assistant, operated by Adam Zeringue · Effective October 20, 2026

Program name and description

Adam's Assistant is a personal automated scheduling assistant operated by Adam Zeringue, an individual. It sends text messages to friends and family who know Adam personally and have agreed to receive texts from his assistant. Messages confirm or arrange the date, time, and place of personal get-togethers, and may include short back-and-forth replies to settle on a time and place. Adam approves each conversation, and the times and places the assistant may offer, before it texts anyone. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

How you agree to receive messages

Adam asks you personally, in person or by text from his own phone, before the assistant ever texts you. The assistant only texts people who have said yes.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Usually 1–10 messages each time a get-together is being planned, and none in between.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, according to your mobile carrier plan.

Help and opting out

Reply HELP for help.

Reply STOP to opt out at any time. You will receive one message confirming that you have opted out, and no further messages will be sent. You can also tell Adam directly that you no longer want texts from his assistant.
